Problems I help businesses fix

If customers can’t find you, they find your competitor. These are the most common visibility problems I work on — and how I approach each one.

01 — LOCAL SEARCH

Not showing up when customers search nearby?

Your Google Business Profile, categories, services, photos and reviews decide whether you appear in Maps and “near me” results. My approach: audit your profile, fix categories and business details, build a local landing-page and citation plan, and set up a simple review workflow.

02 — ORGANIC VISIBILITY

Website gets visits, but few inquiries?

Traffic without action usually means weak service pages, unclear calls-to-action, or pages that don’t match what people actually search for. My approach: keyword and intent research, stronger service pages, internal linking, and clear next steps on every page.

03 — COMPETITORS

Competitors dominate local results?

Competitors ranking above you are doing specific things — better content, stronger profiles, more relevant links. My approach: competitor analysis to find the gaps, then a prioritized plan to close them step by step, honestly and sustainably.

04 — ADS & WEBSITE

Paying for ads or a website that doesn’t convert?

Ad spend and a slow, confusing website waste budget fast. My approach: tighten campaign structure and landing-page alignment, improve site speed and mobile experience, and track what actually leads to an inquiry.
