Problems I help businesses fix

If customers can’t find you, they find your competitor. These are the most common visibility problems I work on — and how I approach each one.

01 — LOCAL SEARCH

Not showing up when customers search nearby?

Your Google Business Profile, categories, services, photos and reviews decide whether you appear in Maps and “near me” results. My approach: audit your profile, fix categories and business details, build a local landing-page and citation plan, and set up a simple review workflow.

02 — ORGANIC VISIBILITY

Website gets visits, but few inquiries?

Traffic without action usually means weak service pages, unclear calls-to-action, or pages that don’t match what people actually search for. My approach: keyword and intent research, stronger service pages, internal linking, and clear next steps on every page.

03 — COMPETITORS

Competitors dominate local results?

Competitors ranking above you are doing specific things — better content, stronger profiles, more relevant links. My approach: competitor analysis to find the gaps, then a prioritized plan to close them step by step, honestly and sustainably.

04 — ADS & WEBSITE

Paying for ads or a website that doesn’t convert?

Ad spend and a slow, confusing website waste budget fast. My approach: tighten campaign structure and landing-page alignment, improve site speed and mobile experience, and track what actually leads to an inquiry.

About Us

About Us

I’m Zain Naseer — an SEO & Local Business Growth Specialist with a BSCS degree and 4 years of SEO experience. I help local businesses worldwide get found on Google, and I got here by ranking the websites I built first.

How it started: a notes app and a lot of missed classes

As a computer science student, I tracked my class attendance by hand in a notes app. It was slow, easy to get wrong, and honestly painful — I never quite knew whether I was safely above the attendance line or one bunk away from trouble.

So I built an attendance calculator to solve that problem. That calculator became my first website, attendancecalculators.net, which now helps students check attendance rules — like the 75% rule — for many universities.

Building that site forced me to learn SEO properly: if students couldn’t find the calculator on Google, it didn’t matter how well it worked. I learned by doing — keyword research, content, technical fixes, and watching what actually moved rankings.

I went on to build and run three more websites: a random Bible verse generator, an elf name generator, and a Turkish Minecraft APK guide site — writing SEO content in both English and Turkish. Two of those websites reached #1 on Google for their own names — “random bible verse generator” and “elf name generator” (verified 25 Oct 2026).

After ranking those sites, I learned Local SEO and started helping local businesses get found on Google — Google Business Profile, local rankings and reviews — alongside SEO, link building, content writing, ads, Pinterest marketing and Amazon affiliate services.

Today I work with clients worldwide, with the US first. I reply within 24 hours, and I always offer a free audit before any paid work — so you know exactly where you stand before you spend anything.
